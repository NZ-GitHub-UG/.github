# Code of Conduct

Welcome to the **Aotearoa New Zealand GitHub User Group**.
Our goal is to create a space where participants can learn, grow, share, and build a community. 
To ensure this, we ask all members to follow these guidelines in all interactions.

## 1. Treat Others with Love and Respect

**Principle:** 
Love your neighbour as yourself

**Practice:** 
Engage with others kindly and respectfully, even in disagreement. 
Avoid personal attacks, insults, or demeaning language. 
Seek to build up rather than tear down.
Remember, we are all learning, and we all had to start somewhere.

## 2. Pursue Truth and Integrity

**Principle:** 
Let your 'Yes' be 'Yes,' and your 'No,' 'No'

**Practice:** 
Share information honestly and accurately.
Give credit where it’s due, avoid plagiarism, and refrain from spreading rumors or falsehoods.

## 3. Practice Humility

**Principle:** 
Do nothing out of selfish ambition or vain conceit. Rather, in humility value others above yourselves

**Practice:**
Celebrate others’ successes, admit when you’re wrong, and be open to learning.
Avoid arrogance or self-promotion at the expense of others.

## 4. Encourage and Support One Another

**Principle:**
Encourage one another and build each other up

**Practice:**
Offer constructive feedback, share knowledge generously, and support members in their tech endeavors. 
Be a source of inspiration and help.

## 5. Maintain Purity in Speech

**Principle:** 
Let no unwholesome talk come out of your mouths, but only what is helpful for building others up

**Practice:**
Avoid profanity, inappropriate jokes, personal or political attacks. 
Use language which edifies and encourages the community.

## 6. Resolve Conflicts Peacefully

**Principle:**
Blessed are the peacemakers

**Practice:**
If disputes arise, address them privately and graciously with the person involved.
Seek reconciliation rather than escalation, and involve moderators if needed.
Be respectful of differing opinions and viewpoints.

## 7. Respect Boundaries and Privacy

**Principle:**
Do to others as you would have them do to you

**Practice:**
Do not share personal information about others without consent.
Respect intellectual property and the privacy of fellow members.
Demonstrating empathy and kindness towards other people.

## Responsibilities

All community members are responsible for adhering to these guidelines, and 
reporting to community leaders any situations where they have been breached
if they cannot be resolved one-to-another. 

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of
acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in
response to any behavior that goes against these guidelines.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject
comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are
not aligned to this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation
decisions when appropriate.

Consequences may include warnings, temporary suspension, or removal from the community, 
depending on the severity and frequency of the offense. 
Our goal is restoration, not punishment, whenever possible.
